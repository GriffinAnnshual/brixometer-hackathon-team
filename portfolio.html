The Dual-Sided Challenge

The sugarcane supply chain suffers from inefficiency at both the farm and the mill.

Farm-Side Crisis

78%

Of farmers rely on visual inspection ("knocking" on cane), leading to inaccurate judgments.

Result: 10–15% Sucrose Loss

Mill-Side Inefficiency

15%

Dispute rate between farmers and mills due to non-transparent quality assessment.

Result: Long queues & Trust Deficit

Technological Innovation

A ground-up hardware and AI solution designed for rugged field use.

Multi-Modal Sensing

  • • AS7265x 6-Channel NIR Spectroscopy
  • • Cross-Polarized Imaging (0° + 90°)
  • • Force Sensor Gating (150-200g)
  • • Ambient Light Compensation

Conformal MoE Model

  • • Mixture of Experts Architecture
  • • Spectral Expert + Rind Texture Expert
  • • Conformal Prediction Sets (90-95%)
  • • Quantized TFLite Micro on Edge

Device Specs

  • • ESP32-S3 + 8MB PSRAM
  • • 3.5" TFT Touchscreen
  • • LiFePO4 Battery (Safe)
  • • IP54 Ruggedized Housing

Actionable Intelligence

Key Features

  • ✔️ Offline-First: Zero network inference.
  • ✔️ Multi-Spot: 3-5 spot averaging per stalk.
  • ✔️ Bilingual UI: Local language + Voice prompts.
  • ✔️ Traceability: QR Code printing integration.
  • ✔️ Mill Integration: TTI Sticker + Backcasting.

Project Timeline

Component procurement, sensor interfacing (I2C/SPI), basic enclosure printing.

Field trials, conformal calibration, mill-side integration pilot.

Phase 3: Months 6-12

Production engineering, injection molding, extensive field deployment.
